# Lanternroom — project brief

Lanternroom is a personal, phone-first AI character chat app (an alternative to Janitor AI) built for its owner, **Fethurz**. It runs entirely in the browser and is published with **GitHub Pages** from this repo.

- Live site: https://fethurz.github.io/Lanternroom/
- **The whole app is one file: `index.html`** (HTML + CSS + JS, no build step, no dependencies besides Google Fonts), plus the installable-app files: `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js` (service worker) and `icons/` (lantern icons: 192, 512, maskable 512, apple-touch 180, svg; rendered by scratch `mkicons.js`).
- **Installable app (PWA, v1.24):** Android Chrome installs it to the home screen (full-screen, own icon, offline start). `sw.js`: the page is network-first (updates land on the next open; cached copy when offline), built-in sounds/icons/fonts cache-first (`lr-media-1`), sound manifests stale-while-revalidate; cross-origin (DeepSeek, Supabase, Pollinations) and Range requests are never touched. Bump `SHELL`/`MEDIA` cache names in `sw.js` only to force-drop old caches. Settings → Look → App shows Install (via `beforeinstallprompt`) or how-to. Back button: `syncNav()` keeps one history entry while a sheet/chat is open (MutationObservers on `#sheet`/`#chat` `hidden`); popstate closes the sound panel, then the sheet, then the chat (`leaveChat()`); exits from Home.
- **Publishing:** commit to `main` → GitHub Pages redeploys in ~1–2 minutes. Phones may cache the old page for a few minutes; the owner checks the version at the bottom of Settings.
- **Bump `VERSION`** (near the top of the script, e.g. `"1.9 · Oct 12"`) with every release so the owner can confirm the update landed.
- The owner uses the app on an Android phone and talks to Claude from the Claude mobile app. Keep explanations short and non-technical; they are not a programmer.

## Owner's rules for Claude (always follow)
- **Never read the owner's chats** (the `c2:` / `chats:` rows in Supabase, or chat contents anywhere) unless the owner has given explicit permission for a specific bot's chat, or Claude asked first and the owner said yes. A "no" is final. Test with made-up chats instead. Bot cards (`chars`), settings and sound lists are fine to read when needed.
- **Content rules are the owner's to write.** The app sends no content restrictions of its own (no forced safe-for-work in images or prompts) except one: characters are always adults (the hidden line in `GEN_SYS`, "Always an adult" in appearance descriptions, "adult" in portrait/illustration prompts). Keep that; add nothing else.

## Owner preferences and conventions
- Mobile first: everything must work at ~390 px wide; tap targets ≥ 34 px.
- Dark, warm look: deep purple-black background, amber accent (`--accent #e8a95b`), fonts Young Serif (display) + Atkinson Hyperlegible (body). The **lantern** is the app's identity.
- Prefers compact layouts, tabs over long scrolling lists, and subtle animation.
- Ships features in batches; test them before pushing (see Testing).
- Adult content is part of the app (vore, scat, sexual sound effects). Build what the owner asks for within Claude's guidelines and say plainly if something can't be done.
